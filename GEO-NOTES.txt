AI search (GEO) — actions that can't be done from the codebase
===============================================================

These notes sit alongside GBP-SEO-NOTES.txt. They cover what makes
Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Apple Intelligence recommend
Toyotech for prompts like "hybrid garage near me in Milton Keynes" or
"where can I get my Prius battery checked in Bletchley".

The website side is in the code: /llms.txt, AI crawlers allowed in
robots.txt, a richer AutoRepair schema, a Service schema on
/hybrid-battery/, and service areas. Everything below needs
Mumthaz's accounts, answers or paperwork.

How the assistants get their facts
-----------------------------------
- Gemini / Google AI Overviews: the Google Business Profile, plus the site.
- ChatGPT search and Microsoft Copilot: mostly Bing's index, so Bing
  Places matters much more than it used to.
- Apple Intelligence / Siri / Apple Maps: Apple Business Connect.
- Perplexity and Claude: their own crawlers plus the web generally.
  Consistent directory citations and descriptive reviews do most of
  the work.
Each one cross-checks name, address and phone (NAP) across sources.
Consistency matters more than quantity.

3. Reviews that tell an AI what you're good at
-----------------------------------------------
AI models read the text of reviews, not just the stars. "Great garage"
helps the rating. "Fixed the hybrid battery on my 2012 Prius and it
passed its MOT" tells the model what Toyotech is good at.

Google's rules (breaking them can get reviews removed or the profile
suspended):
- Don't offer anything in exchange for a review (discount, freebie).
- Don't only ask happy customers ("review gating"). Ask everyone.
- Don't write the review for them or give them text to copy.
  Suggesting what they could mention is fine.
- Staff, family and friends shouldn't review.

Suggested WhatsApp follow-up, sent after every job:

  Hi [name], thanks for bringing your [car] in today. If you have a
  minute, a Google review really helps a small local garage like
  ours: [review link]
  If you can, it helps other drivers to mention your car and what we
  did (for example the MOT, a service or hybrid battery work).
  Thanks, Mumthaz – Toyotech

[ ] Get the direct "write a review" link from GBP (Home → "Ask for
    reviews") and save it as a WhatsApp quick reply.
[ ] Print a small QR card with the review link for the counter and
    for handing over with keys.
[ ] Reply to every review, and name the service in the reply where
    it fits naturally ("Glad the Auris hybrid battery repair sorted
    it"). Replies are read too.
[ ] Once new reviews come in, swap the site's curated quotes
    (src/content/reviews.ts) for ones that mention specific cars and
    jobs, and update the rating/count there.

4. Citations: corroborate the entity everywhere
------------------------------------------------
Use exactly the same name, address, phone and website on every one.
Once a listing is live, send the URL over: it goes into the schema's
sameAs list (src/components/LocalBusinessSchema.astro), which links
the entity together.
Priority (these feed AI assistants directly):
[ ] Bing Places for Business (bingplaces.com). You can import from
    GBP in a few clicks. Feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot.
[ ] Apple Business Connect (businessconnect.apple.com). Claim the
    Apple Maps place card; feeds Siri, Apple Maps and Apple
    Intelligence.
[ ] DVSA MOT station record: check that the registered address and
    trading name match GBP exactly. The "find an MOT centre" service
    uses this.
Automotive directories:
[ ] WhoCanFixMyCar
[ ] BookMyGarage
[ ] Motor Ombudsman garage finder (needs accreditation, see section 1)
[ ] Good Garage Scheme / Trust My Garage (needs membership)
[ ] AutoTrader garage listings (if offered in the area)
[ ] Autoonline / Servicing Stop / ClickMechanic partner listing (optional)
General:
[ ] Yell, Thomson Local, Scoot, Cylex, FreeIndex, Checkatrade,
    Nextdoor business page
[ ] Facebook Business page / Instagram: an entity citation only (the
    site itself deliberately has no social links; these would only
    appear in sameAs if Mumthaz wants them)
[ ] Keep a simple spreadsheet of every listing, its login and the
    exact NAP used, so changes (e.g. hours) get updated everywhere.

5. Getting mentioned elsewhere
-------------------------------
AI models trust businesses that other sites talk about.
[ ] Local press / MK Citizen / MK community Facebook groups: a short
    piece on hybrid battery repair as a cheaper alternative to
    replacement is a real local-interest story.
[ ] Toyota/Lexus/Prius owners' forums and Facebook groups (e.g. Prius
    Owners UK). Being recommended by name in those threads is exactly
    what Perplexity and ChatGPT pick up.
[ ] Local business directories: Milton Keynes Council business
    directory, MK Chamber of Commerce, Bletchley business groups.

7. Checking it's working
-------------------------
- Every couple of months, ask ChatGPT (search on), Gemini,
  Perplexity and Siri: "best hybrid garage in Milton Keynes", "where
  can I get my Prius battery checked in Bletchley", "MOT centre open
  Sunday Bletchley". Note whether Toyotech appears and what it says.
  Fix any wrong facts at the source they came from.
- Bing Webmaster Tools + Google Search Console: verify the domain and
  submit the sitemap (https://toyotech.co.uk/sitemap-index.xml). Bing
  Webmaster Tools also reports on Copilot/AI referrals.
- Check the schema with Google's Rich Results Test and
  validator.schema.org after each deploy that touches it.
- https://toyotech.co.uk/llms.txt should be readable and correct.
