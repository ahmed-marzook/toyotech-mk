Example photos: what good Toyotech website photos look like
===========================================================

This folder is for EXAMPLE images only, to show the style of photo to
take. They are not for the live site. Real photos of the actual workshop
and team always work better for a local garage than stock photos, and
customers can tell the difference.

The shot list itself (file names, where each photo goes) is in
PHOTO-SHOT-LIST.txt.

Note: example images couldn't be downloaded into this folder
automatically, because the environment's network policy blocked the
photo sites. Until then, use the search links below and save any
examples you like into this folder, using the names shown (e.g.
example-hero-1.jpg).

The links go to free stock photo sites (Unsplash and Pexels). Both allow
free use, but only use these as a reference, not on the site.


1. hero.jpg: Home page background (MOST IMPORTANT)
--------------------------------------------------
Sits behind the homepage headline ("Your local garage for MOTs,
servicing and hybrid repairs in Milton Keynes"). It's shown darkened
with white text on top.

Look for:
- Wide shot inside a clean workshop, a car raised on a two-post lift
- Bright, even lighting, bay doors open if possible
- Mechanic working under or beside the car, not posing
- Nothing important on the left side (that's where the text goes)
- Landscape, subject in the centre

Avoid: dark, cluttered benches, close-ups, anything with a visible
number plate or another garage's branding.

Search examples:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/car-lift-garage
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/auto-repair-shop
  https://www.pexels.com/search/car%20workshop%20lift/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/mechanic%20garage/

Save examples as: example-hero-1.jpg, example-hero-2.jpg ...


2. workshop.jpg: Contact page, outside of the garage
----------------------------------------------------
So customers recognise the building when they arrive.

Look for:
- Front of a small independent garage from across the street
- Sign clearly readable, bay doors open, a car or two outside
- Daytime, blue or bright sky, straight on (not a steep angle)

Search examples:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/garage-storefront
  https://www.pexels.com/search/auto%20repair%20shop%20exterior/

Save examples as: example-workshop-1.jpg ...


3. team.jpg: Contact page, the team
-----------------------------------
Look for:
- 2-4 people in work clothes in front of the workshop or a car
- Relaxed and smiling, looking at the camera
- Landscape, everyone in frame with space around them

Search examples:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/mechanic-team
  https://www.pexels.com/search/mechanics%20team/

Save examples as: example-team-1.jpg ...


4. Hybrid work (for Google Business Profile, and a future hybrid page image)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not on the website yet, but good for Google and for showing the hybrid
speciality.

Look for:
- Hybrid battery pack out of a car on a bench, or under a rear seat
- Diagnostic tablet plugged into a car, screen showing live data
- Mechanic in insulated gloves working on orange high-voltage cables
- Toyota Prius / Lexus hybrid in the workshop

Search examples:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/car-diagnostic
  https://www.pexels.com/search/car%20diagnostic/
  https://www.pexels.com/search/hybrid%20car%20battery/

Save examples as: example-hybrid-1.jpg ...


5. Everyday job shots (for Google Business Profile posts)
---------------------------------------------------------
Regular, simple photos keep the Google listing active. Look for:
- Brake disc and caliper close-up with the wheel off
- Engine bay during a service, oil being drained
- A car on the MOT bay / brake roller tester
- Before/after (worn pads vs new pads side by side)

Search examples:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/brake-disc
  https://www.pexels.com/search/car%20service%20oil%20change/

Save examples as: example-jobs-1.jpg ...


TIPS FOR TAKING THE REAL ONES
-----------------------------
- Phone held sideways (landscape), normal camera, full quality.
- Daylight: open the bay doors and turn the workshop lights on too.
- Tidy first: no rubbish, loose tools or clutter.
- Cover customer number plates, no paperwork or screens with customer
  details.
- Ask everyone's permission before they appear in a photo.
- Take 3-4 versions of each shot.
