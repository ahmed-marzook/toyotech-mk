2. WHAT MUMTHAZ WANTS (15 min)
------------------------------
Let Mumthaz talk first. This shapes every other decision.

Business goals
[ ] "What do you want the website to do for you?"
    (More MOTs? More hybrid work? Fewer phone calls? Look more
    professional?)
[ ] "Which jobs do you most want more of?"
    (The most profitable or wanted work goes to the top of the site.)
[ ] "Is there work you don't want, or can't take on?"
[ ] "How busy are you? Can you take more bookings right now?"
    (Affects how hard the site pushes "same-day bookings".)

Customers
[ ] "Who are your typical customers, and how do they find you today?"
    (Google, word of mouth, passing trade, taxi drivers, fleets?)
[ ] "Which areas do customers come from?"
    Site currently lists: Bletchley, Fenny Stratford, Water Eaton,
    Central Milton Keynes, Wolverton, Stony Stratford, Newport
    Pagnell, Milton Keynes.
[ ] "Do many customers prefer a language other than English?"
[ ] "What do customers ask most often on the phone or WhatsApp?"
    (These become FAQ content.)

Look and feel
[ ] "Any garage or competitor sites whose look you like?"
[ ] "Any brand rules beyond the logo, e.g. a font?"
    (Site uses the red and navy from the signage.)
[ ] "Do you have a clean logo file with a transparent background?"
